# 3D prototype assets

All from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0 (public domain). No attribution required; credited here anyway.

- sky/limpopo_golf_course_2k.hdr: "Limpopo Golf Course" HDRI
- aerial_grass_rock/: "Aerial Grass Rock" texture (diffuse, normal, roughness, 1k)
- aerial_sand/: "Aerial Sand" texture (diffuse, normal, 1k)
- bark_willow/: "Bark Willow" texture (diffuse, normal, 1k)
- shrub_04/: "Shrub 04" model (glTF, 1k textures)

## Trees (made for Ink Nine)

- trees/oak_a.glb, oak_b.glb, birch.glb, pine.glb and trees/leaf_atlas.png were grown in Blender by desktop/3d/tools/trees_blender.py, with soft shading (ambient occlusion) worked out per point and stored in vertex colours. The game textures the bark with "Bark Willow" above.
